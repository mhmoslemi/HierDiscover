Implementation of a softmax‑Adam optimizer for the step‑function
self‑convolution bound.  The variables live on the probability simplex
via a softmax parametrisation, a sub‑gradient of the active convolution

    """Numerically stable softmax."""
    z_max = np.max(z)
    e = np.exp(z - z_max)

    old_n = len(seq)

    x_old = np.linspace(0.0, 1.0, old_n)
    x_new = np.linspace(0.0, 1.0, new_n)

    """

    best_score = np.inf

    lengths = [1400, 2200, 3000, 3800, 4600]

            if time.time() >= deadline:
