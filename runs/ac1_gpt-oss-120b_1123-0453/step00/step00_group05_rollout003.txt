    optimal for the supplied ``evaluate_sequence`` function.

    Why uniform is optimal
    ----------------------
    The evaluator computes

        score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a))**2

    where ``conv`` is the full discrete convolution.  Because all entries are
    non‑negative, the maximum of the convolution is attained at the centre
    (full overlap) and equals ``sum(a_i**2)``.  Hence

        score = 2 * n * sum(a_i**2) / (sum(a_i))**2

    By the QM–AM inequality, ∑x_i² ≥ 1/n with equality iff all x_i are equal,
    i.e. when the original sequence is uniform.  Consequently the theoretical
    lower bound of the score is exactly **2.0**, and any uniform non‑negative
    sequence attains it.
