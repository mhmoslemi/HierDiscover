scale‑invariant).  The maximal convolution then occurs at the centre

    max_b = 1000^2 * n * Beta(p+1, p+1),

where Beta is the Beta‑function.  The resulting score is

    score = 2 * (p+1)^2 * Beta(p+1, p+1).

Choosing p=6 gives a theoretical score ≈ 0.008, far below the target
1.503.  The construction is O(n) and needs only one evaluation, so it
comfortably fits the time budget.

The function ignore the random seed / budget because the deterministic
construction already beats the required bound.
