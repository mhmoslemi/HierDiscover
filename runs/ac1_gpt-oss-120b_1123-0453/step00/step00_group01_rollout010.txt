"""
Propose a step‑function sequence that provably improves the evaluation score.

The evaluation of a sequence a of length n is

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

where conv(a, a) is the linear convolution of a with itself.
Because the score is homogeneous (scaling a does not change the score),
we may normalise the sequence so that sum(a) = 1.  Then

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)).

For a uniform sequence a_i = 1/n we have max(conv) = 1/n and score = 2.
A simple construction with three equal‑size blocks,
each containing a constant value, achieves a strictly smaller
maximum of the convolution and therefore a lower score.

Let L be a positive integer and build the sequence

    [ 0.5/L, ..., 0.5/L ]          (L times)
    [ 0.25/L, ..., 0.25/L ]        (L times)
    [ 0.25/L, ..., 0.25/L ]        (L times)

The total length is n = 3·L and the sum of the sequence is
( L·0.5/L + L·0.25/L + L·0.25/L ) = 1.
The convolution consists of three self‑terms and three cross‑terms.
All of them are equal to 0.25/L, hence

    max(conv) = 0.25 / L,
    score = 2 * (3·L) * (0.25 / L) = 1.5.

Thus the constructed sequence attains a score of exactly 1.5,
