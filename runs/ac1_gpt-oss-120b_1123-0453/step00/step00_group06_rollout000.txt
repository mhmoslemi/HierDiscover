"""

--------------------
For any non‑negative sequence `a` the evaluator computes

    Q = 2 * n * max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a))**2

Because `a_i >= 0`, the maximum of the self‑convolution is always attained at
lag 0:

    max(conv(a,a)) = Σ a_i².

Hence

    Q = 2 * n * Σ a_i² / (Σ a_i)²

identical.  Therefore the theoretical minimum of `Q` is exactly **2**, and it
is achieved by any constant (uniform) sequence with at least one positive
entry.
