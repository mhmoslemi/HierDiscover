For any non‑negative sequence a the maximum of the convolution is

inequality

    (sum a)^2 ≤ n * sum a^2  ⇒  2 * n * sum a^2 / (sum a)^2 ≥ 2

with equality **iff** all entries are equal.  Hence a constant
sequence is provably optimal and yields the value 2, which is the
global minimum.
