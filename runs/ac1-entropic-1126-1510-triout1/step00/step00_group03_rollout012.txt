Observation
-----------
The evaluation function simplifies (after the mandatory clipping) to

    E(a) = 2 * n * max_k (p * p)_k   with   p_i = a_i / sum(a)

score is obtained after the clipping.

available length `n` (taken from the global `height_sequence_1` if it exists)
