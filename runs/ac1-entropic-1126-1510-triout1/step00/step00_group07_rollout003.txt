The evaluation function is homogeneous:
 E(a) = 2·n·max_k (a∗a)_k / (∑a)^2 .

For a non‑negative vector a of length n the quantity
 max_k (a∗a)_k

Choosing a linear decreasing profile
 a_i ∝ (n‑i)   (i = 0,…,n‑1)

 E = 4·(n+2) / (3·(n+1))  → 4/3 ≈ 1.333…

The function below builds this profile, normalises it to a total
