Simple optimizer for the step‑function evaluation.

The objective to minimise is

possible score is exactly **2**, achieved by any constant (uniform) sequence.

optimal in theory but also safe for the implementation.  The function below
returns such a sequence (reusing the length of the previously known construction
if it is available, otherwise falling back to a modest length).

The routine prints the achieved score so that the caller can see the result.
"""
