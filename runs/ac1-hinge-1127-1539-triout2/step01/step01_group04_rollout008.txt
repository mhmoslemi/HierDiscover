The algorithm works in three phases:

1. **Smart initialisation**
   – If a previous construction ``height_sequence_1`` is available it is taken,
     otherwise a short sweep over a few parametric families (sqrt‑diff,
     power‑law and exponential) is performed and the best shape (with
     L1‑norm √(2 n)) is kept.

2. **Weighted‑gradient descent (Adam)**
   – At each iteration the self‑convolution is obtained with a single FFT.
   – The infinity‑norm is approximated by a soft‑max:
         w_k = exp(α·(conv_k – max(conv))
     with a temperature α that grows from 0.5 to 30.
   – The gradient of the (soft) maximum is the weighted sum of the usual
     sub‑gradients:   ∂/∂a_i  Σ_k w_k·(a*a)_k = 2·Σ_k w_k·a_{k‑i}.

3. **Exploratory jitter**

# Helper: FFT‑based self‑convolution (full length 2·n‑1)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
def _self_conv_fft(a: np.ndarray, L: int) -> np.ndarray:
    """Return the full self‑convolution of a using an FFT of length L."""
    fa = np.fft.rfft(a, n=L)
    conv = np.fft.irfft(fa * fa, n=L)[: 2 * a.size - 1]
    return conv

    """Return a list of candidate arrays (already normalised)."""
    idx = np.arange(n, dtype=np.float64)

        s = a.sum()
        if s == 0:
