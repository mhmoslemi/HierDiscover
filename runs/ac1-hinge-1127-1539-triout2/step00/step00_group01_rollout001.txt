Uniform baseline for the step‑function minimisation problem.

The evaluation function

    evaluate_sequence(seq) = 2 * n * max(conv(seq, seq)) / (sum(seq))**2

Indeed, for non‑negative vectors the maximum of the convolution is attained

∑ a_i² ≥ (∑ a_i)² / n, with equality iff the vector is uniform.
