Theory
------
For a non‑negative vector a with sum(a)=1 the score returned by

    score = 2·n·max_k (a * a)[k]                     (1)

    a_i ∝ (n‑i)   , i = 0 … n‑1

    max_k (a * a)[k] =  n (n+1) (n+2) / (6·S²)       (2)
