"""
Catalan‐based construction.

The convolution of the Catalan numbers  C_k = binom(2k,k)/4**k  satisfies
    Σ_i C_i·C_{k-i} = 1   for every k ≥ 0.
Hence the (non‑normalised) sequence a_k = C_k has a perfectly flat
self–convolution (value 1) in the interior of the array.
If we take the first n Catalan numbers as a step‑function,
the sum S_n grows like (2/√π)·√n, while the maximum of the convolution
stays equal to 1.  Consequently

    evaluate_sequence(a_0,…,a_{n-1}) = 2·n / S_n² → π/2 ≈ 1.571

as n → ∞, which is a substantial improvement over the trivial uniform
construction (value 2.0).  The implementation below builds the Catalan
vector of the same length as the previously discovered construction
