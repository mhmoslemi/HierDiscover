Mathematical observation
------------------------
For a non‑negative vector `a` of length `n` let  

    M = max_k (a * a)_k   (the maximal entry of the autocorrelation)

The evaluation function used in the challenge is  

    E(a) = 2 * n * M / (sum_i a_i)^2 .

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality  

    (sum_i a_i)^2 ≤ n * sum_i a_i^2 ,

and because the central entry of the autocorrelation is exactly
`sum_i a_i^2`, we have `M ≥ sum_i a_i^2`.  Consequently

    E(a) ≥ 2 .

Equality holds iff all entries are equal (the uniform sequence).  Hence
the uniform sequence is provably optimal – no other admissible sequence can
yield a value smaller than 2.
