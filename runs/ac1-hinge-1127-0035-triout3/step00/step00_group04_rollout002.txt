    a_i ∝ 1/√(x_i (1‑x_i)) with x_i = (i+0.5)/n.
    The vector is normalised so that its ℓ1‑norm equals 1.

    i = np.arange(n, dtype=np.float64)

    a = np.clip(a, 0, 1e6)

    return a
