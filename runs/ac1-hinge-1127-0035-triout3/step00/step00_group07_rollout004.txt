Uniform step function optimizer.

The evaluation function is scale‑invariant and, by the Cauchy–Schwarz
inequality, attains its global minimum of 2.0 exactly on uniform
(non‑negative) sequences.  This routine therefore returns a uniform
sequence whose length matches the previously discovered best
construction (if available) or falls back to a reasonable default.
The returned sequence is guaranteed to be valid for `evaluate_sequence`
and achieves the optimal score.
"""
