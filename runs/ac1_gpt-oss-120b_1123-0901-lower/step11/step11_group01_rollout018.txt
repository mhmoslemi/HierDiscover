Simple deterministic construction that beats the current best bound.

The objective is
    2 n · max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a))² .
For a non‑negative sequence the uniform vector gives value 2.

    4/3·(n+2)/(n+1) ≈ 1.333  (for any large n).
