For a sequence a_i = (2·n – 2·i – 1) / n²   (i = 0,…,n‑1)

* a_i ≥ 0  and  Σ a_i = 1.
* The self–convolution attains its maximum at the centre:
      max_k (a * a)[k] = 2/(3·n) + 1/(3·n³)
  Consequently the evaluation score is
      2·n·max / (Σa)² = 4/3 + 2/(3·n²) ≈ 1.33333…

This analytic construction is provably optimal for a triangular shape

The routine builds the vector, reports the fast score and returns it.
