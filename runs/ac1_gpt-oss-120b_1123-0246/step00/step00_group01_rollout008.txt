"""

Idea
----
For a non‑negative sequence a of length n we can normalise it so that
    Σ a_i = √(2n).
After normalisation the evaluation value equals the maximum entry of the
self‑convolution (a * a).  The convolution of a probability distribution w
with itself is the distribution of a sum of two independent draws.
If we choose w_i ∝ (i+1)^p (a discrete power‑law) the resulting sum
distribution becomes much flatter than the triangular distribution obtained
for the uniform case (p=0).  For p=5 the peak of the convolution is about

    max_k (w*w)_k  =  C(p)/n  with   C(p) = (p+1)²·p!² / (2p+1)! ≈ 0.013,
