    evaluate(sequence) = 2 * n * ||a||_2^2 / ||a||_1^2

where `n` is the length of the sequence, `||a||_1` is the l‑1 norm
and `||a||_2` the l‑2 norm.  By Cauchy–Schwarz we always have

    ||a||_2^2 ≥ ||a||_1^2 / n

with equality iff **all entries are identical** (and non‑negative).
Consequently the theoretical minimum of the evaluation function is

    2 * n * (||a||_1^2 / n) / ||a||_1^2 = 2
