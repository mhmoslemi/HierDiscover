Uniform‑step search.

The evaluation function for a non‑negative step sequence is

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

For any non‑negative vector a, the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives

    max(conv(a, a)) ≥ sum_i a_i² ≥ (sum_i a_i)² / n

included as a safety net – it may discover a marginally better value caused
