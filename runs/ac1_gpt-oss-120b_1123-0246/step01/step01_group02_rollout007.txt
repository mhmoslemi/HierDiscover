"""
optimized_two_block.py – deterministic construction beating the π/2 bound.

The idea:
    * Let n be the length of the step function.
    * Choose two equal‑sized blocks of non‑zero height and place a gap of at
      least one zero between them (i.e. one block at the start, one at the end).
    * Set the height h so that the total sum of the sequence equals 1:
          2·L·h = 1   ⇒   h = 1 / (2·L)
      where L = ⌊(n‑1)/2⌋ is the maximal block length that still leaves a gap.
    * With this shape the self‑convolution has at most L overlapping terms,
      hence max (a * a) = L·h² = 1/(4·L).  The evaluation function becomes

          score = 2·n·max_b / (sum_a)² = 2·n·(1/(4·L)) = n/(2·L),

      which for L≈n/2 gives a score arbitrarily close to 1 (≈1.00036 for n=5582),
      well below the target 1.503.
