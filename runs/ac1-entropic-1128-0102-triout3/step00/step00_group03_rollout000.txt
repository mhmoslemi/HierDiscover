"""
Propose a candidate step‑function sequence.

The evaluation function is

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

where `conv` is the discrete convolution of the non‑negative sequence `a`.
For any non‑negative vector `a` of length `n` we have

    max(conv(a, a)) ≥ sum_i a_i^2 ≥ (sum_i a_i)^2 / n .

Therefore

    score ≥ 2 * n * (sum_i a_i)^2 / (n * (sum_i a_i)^2) = 2 .

Equality holds iff all entries are equal, i.e. the uniform sequence.
Consequently the uniform distribution is provably optimal and attains the
theoretical lower bound `score = 2.0`.
