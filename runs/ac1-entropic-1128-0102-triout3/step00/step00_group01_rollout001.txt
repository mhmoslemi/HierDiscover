    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

    max(conv(a, a)) ≥ sum_i a_i^2 ≥ (sum_i a_i)^2 / n

so the score is bounded from below by 2, and equality is attained
exactly when all entries are equal.  Therefore a uniform sequence
