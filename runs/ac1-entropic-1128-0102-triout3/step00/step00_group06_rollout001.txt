Simple provably optimal solution for the step‑function bound.

The evaluation function is

    E(a) = 2 * n * max( a * a ) / ( sum(a) ** 2 )

where `max(a*a)` is the maximum entry of the full convolution of `a`
with itself.  For non‑negative entries this maximum is always attained at
the centre of the convolution and equals the sum of squares
∑ a_i².  Hence

    E(a) = 2 * n * (∑ a_i²) / (∑ a_i)².

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality   (∑ a_i)² ≤ n·∑ a_i²,
so   E(a) ≥ 2, with equality exactly when all entries are equal.
Therefore the optimum is 2, attained by any constant (uniform) sequence.

The algorithm simply returns a uniform sequence of a reasonable length.
If a previously found sequence `height_sequence_1` is already available and
its score is optimal (≈ 2), it is reused; otherwise a fresh uniform
sequence is created.
"""
