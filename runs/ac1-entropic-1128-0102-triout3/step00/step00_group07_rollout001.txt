    E(a) = 2 * n * max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a)^2)

satisfies the inequality  E(a) >= 2 for every non‑negative sequence a.
Proof:
    * The maximum of the full convolution is attained at zero shift and equals
      ∑ a_i^2.
    * By Cauchy‑Schwarz, ∑ a_i^2 >= (∑ a_i)^2 / n.

Hence the lower bound 2 is tight and is achieved exactly when all entries are
equal (the “uniform” step function).  Consequently no search can beat the
score 2, and the optimal construction is simply a constant vector.

The function below returns such a uniform sequence.  It uses the length of the
previous best construction (``height_sequence_1``) when that variable is
available; otherwise it defaults to a moderate size (1000).  A short sanity
check prints the achieved score before returning the sequence.
