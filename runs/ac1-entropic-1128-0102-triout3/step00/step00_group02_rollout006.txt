A deterministic construction for the step–function problem.

The idea:
    • Choose a monomial density f(x) = (p+1)·x^p on [0,1].
      Its auto‑convolution satisfies   max(f*f) = (p+1)^2·B(p+1,p+1)
      where B is the beta function.  Consequently

          evaluate_sequence ≈ 2·(p+1)^2·(p!²)/(2p+1)! ,

      which rapidly decreases with the exponent p.
    • Discretise the density on a grid of size n:
          a_i = (i+1)^p ,   i = 0,…,n‑1
      (these are non‑negative, sum > 0 and the shape mimics the continuous f).
    • The evaluation is scale‑invariant, but the provided
      `evaluate_sequence` clamps all entries to ≤1000.
      Therefore we simply scale the whole vector so that the
      maximum entry equals 1000:
          scale = 1000 / n^p .
