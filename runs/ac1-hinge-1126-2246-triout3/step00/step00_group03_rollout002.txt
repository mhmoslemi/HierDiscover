The evaluation function is

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a)**2)

where `conv` is the ordinary (linear) convolution.
For a non‑negative vector `a` the maximal convolution entry is always
the centre term `∑ a_i²`.  Hence

    score = 2 * n * (∑ a_i²) / (∑ a_i)².

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality   (∑ a_i)² ≤ n·∑ a_i²,
and equality holds exactly when all entries are equal.
Therefore the minimal possible score is **2**, attained by any uniform
(non‑zero) sequence.  No amount of randomisation, linear‑programming or
search can beat this bound.
