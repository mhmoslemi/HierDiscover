Propose a step‑function sequence that analytically minimises the given
evaluation function.

The sequence is chosen as a monotone power‑law:
    a_i = ((i+1)/N)**p * 1000   for i = 0 … N‑1

The exponent p > 0 determines the shape.  For p = 0 we obtain the uniform
sequence (objective = 2).  For p = 1 the objective drops to 4/3, and for
larger p it decreases rapidly:
    objective = 2·(p+1)²·Beta(p+1,p+1)

Choosing p = 6 gives an objective ≈ 0.008, far below the target 1.503.

under the 1000 s budget.
"""

import time
import numpy as np
