"""
Geometric‑decay construction (no search).

The evaluation function is scale‑invariant:
if all entries are multiplied by a constant the value of
evaluate_sequence does not change.  Hence we may normalise the
L1‑norm of the sequence to 1 without loss of optimality.

For a geometric sequence a_i = r**i (i = 0,…,n‑1) the largest
convolution entry is either at the left edge (a_0²) or at the
centre (≈ n·r^{n‑1}·a_0²).  Choosing r so that these two candidates
are balanced, i.e.  n·r^{n‑1}=1, minimises the maximum convolution.
The solution of this equation is

    r = exp( -log(n) / (n‑1) ).

With this r the maximal autocorrelation is roughly (1‑r)²,
and the score returned by evaluate_sequence is

    2·n·(1‑r)²  ≈  2·(log n)² / n,

which quickly falls below 0.1 for n in the few‑thousand range.
The construction therefore produces a very small score without any
iterative optimisation or linear programming.

The function below builds such a sequence, normalises it to L1=1,
and returns it.  It runs in a fraction of a second even for n≈5000,
