    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a)^2)

For any non‑negative sequence `a` the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality guarantees

        score = evaluate_sequence(best_seq)
    except Exception as exc:  # pragma: no cover
        print("Error during evaluation:", exc)
        score = float('inf')

    print(f"Proposed sequence length: {len(best_seq)}   evaluation score: {score:.6f}")

    return best_seq
