"""
Propose a near‑optimal step‐function sequence.

The evaluation function is scale‑invariant:
    E(a) = 2 * n * max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a)^2)

For any non‑negative sequence the central convolution term equals
∑ a_i^2, and by Cauchy–Schwarz   ∑ a_i^2 ≥ (∑ a_i)^2 / n.
Hence

    E(a) ≥ 2 * n * (∑ a_i)^2 / (n * (∑ a_i)^2) = 2.

The bound 2 is attained by a *uniform* sequence (all entries equal).
Therefore the best possible score is exactly 2.0, and any uniform
sequence of any length satisfies it.

The proposer below quickly verifies this bound, tries a few random
lengths, and finally returns a uniform sequence (length 1457 to match
