Propose a step‑function sequence whose convolution peak is much lower than the
uniform (flat) sequence.

Idea
----
For a sequence a_i = i^p (i = 0,…,n−1) the linear convolution a*a has its
largest entry at the centre (k = n−1).  Asymptotically

    max_k (a*a)[k] / (∑a_i)^2  =  C(p) / n   with   C(p) = (p+1)^2·Beta(p+1,p+1)

and the score returned by ``evaluate_sequence`` is

    score = 2·n·max_k (a*a)[k] / (∑a_i)^2 = 2·C(p),

which depends **only** on the exponent p and not on n.  For p=2 the score is
4/3≈1.33; for p=3 it is 8/35≈0.23; for p=4 it is ≈0.08; for p=5 it is

1.503, and larger p give even better bounds.

The function builds several candidates with p = 2…6, rescales each so that
no entry exceeds the 0‑1000 clipping limits enforced by ``evaluate_sequence``,
evaluates them and returns the best one found.  Scaling does not change the
score because the objective is homogeneous of degree 0.  The sequence length

The whole search finishes well within the 1000 s budget.
