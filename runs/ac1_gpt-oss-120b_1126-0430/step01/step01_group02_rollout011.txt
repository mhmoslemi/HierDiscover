approximation of ``max(a*a)`` (log‑sum‑exp) together with a small L2‑flattening

    projection.  Random restarts and a decreasing temperature (γ) are employed
    to escape poor local minima.  The best sequence observed before the time
    limit is returned.

    deadline = time.time() + budget_s - 2.0   # small safety margin

    # problem size – a few thousand entries give a good trade‑off
    n = 2500

    a = _catalan_sequence(n)

    beta2 = 0.999

    gamma = 5.0
    gamma_max = 200.0
    gamma_inc = 1.02

    avg = 1.0 / (2 * n - 1)

        # ----- convolution and the two gradient contributions -----
        conv = np.convolve(a, a)                      # length 2n‑1

        #   w_k = exp(γ (conv_k - maxc))  (stable version)
