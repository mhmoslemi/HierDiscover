The evaluation metric is

    2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

where ``conv`` is the linear convolution of the sequence with itself.

value is attained for the centre shift (i.e. the inner product of ``a`` with

    score(p) = 2·(p+1)²·Beta(p+1, p+1),
