Uniform‑distribution proposer
-----------------------------

The evaluation function is

    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

where `conv(a, a)` is the full discrete self‑convolution of a non‑negative
vector `a` of length `n`.  Because the convolution at lag 0 equals
`∑ a_i²`, the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality gives

    max(conv) ≥ ∑ a_i² ≥ (∑ a_i)² / n .

Consequently

    score ≥ 2 * n * (∑ a_i)² / n / (∑ a_i)² = 2 .

The bound is tight and is attained exactly for a **uniform** vector,
i.e. `a_i = 1/n` for all `i`.  Hence the globally optimal step function
(up to the harmless scaling invariance of the objective) is the uniform
distribution.

The `propose_candidate` function below simply returns this optimum.
If a previous construction `height_sequence_1` is present we reuse its
length; otherwise we fall back to the length used in the reference
implementation (5146).  The routine runs in well under the 1000 s budget
and prints a short diagnostic.
