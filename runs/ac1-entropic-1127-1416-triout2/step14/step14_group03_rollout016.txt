Simple analytic construction for the step‑function minimisation problem.

The evaluation function is
    score = 2·n·max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a)**2)

If we normalise the coefficients to sum = 1 the denominator cancels and the
objective becomes 2·n·max(conv(a,a)).  For a vector

    a_i ∝ (i+1)^p   (i = 0 … n‑1)

the convolution at the centre (k = n‑1) can be evaluated analytically:

    max(conv) = C(p) / n   with   C(p) = (p+1)^2·Γ(p+1)^2 / Γ(2p+2)

and the edge diagonals give at most ((p+1)/n)^2.  By choosing a moderate
exponent (p = 6) the centre dominates and

    score = 2·C(6) ≈ 0.0239
