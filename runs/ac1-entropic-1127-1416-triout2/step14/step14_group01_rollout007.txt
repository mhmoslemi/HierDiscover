Triangular step-function generator.
Constructs a decreasing linear sequence (a discrete triangle) that minimises the
autocorrelation peak—its theoretical objective value converges to 4/3 ≈ 1.333.

def triangle_sequence(n: int) -> np.ndarray:
    """Return a length‑n non‑negative vector whose entries form a discrete
    triangle, i.e. a_i = 2*(n-1-i) / (n*(n-1)).  The vector sums to 1 and ends
    with a zero entry."""

    a = np.maximum(a, 0.0)
    # normalise to avoid floating‑point drift
    a /= a.sum()
