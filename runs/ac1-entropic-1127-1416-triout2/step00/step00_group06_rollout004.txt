Propose a candidate step‑function sequence.

The evaluation function is scale‑invariant:
    score = 2·n·max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a))**2 .
If we fix the L¹‑norm to √(2·n), the score equals the

    2·(p+1)²·B(p+1,p+1) = 2·(p+1)²·(p!·p!)/(2p+1)!   (B = beta function)

The construction below builds such a ramp, normalises it so that
