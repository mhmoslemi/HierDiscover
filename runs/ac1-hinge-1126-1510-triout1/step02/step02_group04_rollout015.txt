    2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) .
For a monotone power‑law a_i ∝ i^p (i = 1 … n) the maximal convolution
occurs at the centre and equals

    max(conv) = 2 * (p+1)² * B(p+1, p+1) / n

where B is the Beta‑function.  The resulting bound is
    2 * n * max(conv) = 2 * (p+1)² * B(p+1, p+1)
