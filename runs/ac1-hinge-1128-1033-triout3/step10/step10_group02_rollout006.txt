    analytically minimizes the evaluation function. The sequence a_i = 2*(n-i)/(n*(n+1))

A fast deterministic construction for the step‑function attack.

The sequence is a simple decreasing linear “triangle”:

    a_i = 2·(n‑i) / (n·(n+1)),   i = 0 … n‑1

    max(conv(a,a)) = 2·(n+2) / (3·n·(n+1))
