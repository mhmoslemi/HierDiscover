Simulated‑annealing search for a low‑peak self‑convolution.

The objective returned by ``evaluate_sequence`` is
    2·n·max_k (a*a)[k] / (sum(a))² .
Because the ratio is scale‑invariant we fix ``sum(a)=1`` and minimise
    F(a) = 2·n·max_k (a*a)[k] .
The algorithm works directly on the time domain:

*   Initialise a (optionally from the global ``height_sequence_1``) and
    normalise it to sum = 1.
*   Compute the full convolution ``conv = a*a`` once.
*   Repeatedly propose a small transfer of mass ``δ`` from an index *i*
    that contributes most to the current peak (the index where the
    convolution attains its maximum) to a randomly chosen index *j*.
    The convolution can be updated in O(n) by exploiting the linearity of
    the convolution with respect to each coefficient.
*   Accept the move if the peak does not increase; otherwise accept it

    ``T`` is linearly cooled from a few times the initial peak down to
    virtually zero.
*   Keep the best sequence seen so far and report it when the time budget
    (default 1000 s) expires.

The whole optimisation runs inside the time budget, prints occasional
