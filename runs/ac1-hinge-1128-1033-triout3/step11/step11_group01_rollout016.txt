Alternative optimiser for the step‑function bound.

The algorithm works with a soft‑max (logits) representation so that all
heights stay non‑negative and sum to one.  At each iteration we:

  1. Convert logits → probabilities a.
  2. Compute the *exact* self‑convolution a*a by FFT.
  3. Identify the (possibly several) indices attaining the maximal
     convolution entry.
  4. Form a one‑hot (or uniform over ties) mask w and obtain the
     sub‑gradient of the true max:  ∇_a max = 2·cross‑corr(w, a).
  5. Back‑propagate through the soft‑max, treat the result as a gradient
     for the logits and apply an Adam update.
  6. Occasionally add Gaussian noise to the logits when progress stalls.

The best sequence seen during the 1000 s time budget is finally
evaluated with `evaluate_sequence` and returned.
