The objective function  

\[
E(a)=\frac{2\,n\,\max\limits_{k}(a*a)[k]}{\bigl(\sum_i a_i\bigr)^2},
\]

where \((a*a)[k]=\sum_i a_i\,a_{i+k}\) is the discrete auto‑convolution,
has a simple mathematical lower bound.

For any non‑negative vector \(a\)

* the term at shift \(k=0\) is \(\sum_i a_i^2\);
* by Cauchy‑Schwarz,
  \(\displaystyle\sum_i a_i^2\;\ge\;\frac{(\sum_i a_i)^2}{n}\).

Therefore  

\[
\max_k (a*a)[k]\;\ge\;\sum_i a_i^2\;\ge\;\frac{(\sum_i a_i)^2}{n}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E(a)\;\ge\;2 .
\]

Equality is attained when **all entries are equal** (the uniform step
function).  Consequently the best possible score is exactly 2 and it is
unimprovable under the given non‑negativity and clipping rules.

The safest way to return an optimal construction is therefore to output
a uniform sequence of any positive value.  The concrete length does not
matter – the evaluation is scale‑invariant – but we keep the length
