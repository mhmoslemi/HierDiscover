Uniform sequence optimizer.

The evaluation function simplifies to:
    score = 2 * n * max(conv(a, a)) / (sum(a) ** 2)

For a non‑negative sequence `a` the maximum of the linear convolution is always
`max(conv(a, a)) = sum_i a_i**2`.  Hence

    score = 2 * n * (sum_i a_i**2) / (sum_i a_i)**2.

By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality,
    sum_i a_i**2 ≥ (sum_i a_i)**2 / n,
with equality iff all entries are equal (uniform).

Therefore the minimal possible score is exactly `2`, attained by any uniform
