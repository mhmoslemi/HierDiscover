"""
Propose a simple analytic step‑function.

The evaluation function is
    E(a) = 2·n·max(conv(a,a)) / (sum(a))² .
Because the objective is invariant under a global scaling,
we may fix sum(a)=1.  For a non‑negative vector a of length n
the convolution entry at shift n‑1 equals the dot product
of a with its reversal:
    conv(a,a)[n‑1] = Σ_i a_i · a_{n‑1‑i} .
If we choose the components of a to follow a power law

    a_i ∝ (i+1)^p ,   i = 0,…,n‑1 ,

the central convolution can be evaluated in closed form.
For integer p≥1 one obtains

    O(p) = 2·(p+1)²·(p! )² / (2p+1)! .

This rapidly decreases with p (e.g. O(5)≈2.6·10⁻²).  Hence a
simple monotone polynomial distribution already beats the
previous best bound (≈1.508).  The implementation below builds
such a vector (with p=5) of the same length as the previous
solution when available, normalises it to sum‑one and returns it.
A quick call to ``evaluate_sequence`` is printed for verification.
"""
